How to play

  • Cross the roads without getting hit.
  • Rivers are deadly: ride the logs across.
  • Fill every goal slot to clear the stage.
  • Chain coins for an x5 combo.
  • 3 lives, 30 seconds per attempt.
Started life as a 42 School so_long project, rebuilt as an arcade game.
